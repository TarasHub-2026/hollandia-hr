Hollandia Greenhouses HR Management

Install on your iPhone in 4 steps

  1. Point your iPhone camera at the code and tap the link. It opens in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Open the app from the new icon.
Sign in with your username (or employee number or email) and PIN. New here? Tap Create Profile and use your employee number, email and hire date.
Forgot your PIN? Tap "Forgot your username or PIN?" on the sign-in screen, or ask HR.
If the camera does not open the link, ask HR for help. Install must be done from Safari, not Chrome.
