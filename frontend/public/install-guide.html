Hollandia Greenhouses HR Management

Install the app on your phone: apply for leave and loans, read announcements, and message HR

Download QR code (image) Download this guide
Scan this code with your phone camera, or open this link:
https://tarashub-2026.github.io/hollandia-hr/

It is a web app, so there is nothing to download from an app store. Follow the steps below to put it on your home screen like any other app.

iPhone

Use Safari (the blue compass). It will not install from Chrome.

  1. Point your camera at the code and tap the link, or open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add (top right). Open the app from the new Hollandia HR icon.

If you do not see Add to Home Screen, tap View More in the Share sheet, or open the link in Safari rather than from inside another app.

Android

Use Chrome (works on Samsung, Pixel and most phones).

  1. Point your camera at the code and tap the link, or open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then tap Install.
  4. Open the app from the new Hollandia HR icon on your home screen or app list.

Samsung Internet: tap the menu (three lines), then Add page to, then Home screen.

Sign in with your username (or employee number or email) and PIN. New here? Tap Create Profile and use your employee number, email and hire date.
Forgot your PIN? Tap “Forgot your username or PIN?” on the sign-in screen, or ask HR.
Alerts: when the app asks to send notifications, tap Allow so you hear about leave and loan decisions straight away. On iPhone, alerts only work once the app has been added to the Home Screen.
Updates: the app updates itself. If something looks out of date, close it completely and open it again.
Need help? Ask HR. You can also use the app in any browser at the link above without installing it.
